REVISION 23 - LOADED-CRAFT BUOYANCY DAMPING
Replace the previous combined addon folder, fully restart and reconnect.

The prior damping used the buoyancy compartment's own mass. A compartment attached
to a much heavier vessel therefore received insufficient damping for vessel heave.
Now groups submerged active compartments by VSS baseplate, reads the framework's
CFW_GetContraption().totalMass, and computes a shared exponential damping budget
from that mass and combined waterline stiffness. Distributes it by compartment
stiffness over the wet sample points. Adding identical total capacity in more
compartments does not multiply the damping budget. Resistance fades at first
water contact and leaves lift capacity, health, Wire ratios and lateral drag alone.

If the framework mass is unavailable, falls back to baseplate physics mass plus
active submerged compartment masses. That fallback cannot account for the rest
of the boat. No constraints.GetAll or global entity scan is added per water tick.
Native-water, immovable and dry compartments do not enter the damping group.
VSS naval eligibility is still enforced in the existing force application path.

Tests: actual water hook settles a simulated 50,000kg rigid boat with 100,000kg
capacity at 30/66/100Hz. Splitting equal capacity across one or four compartments
produces the same heave response in this simulation. Existing displacement,
health/baseplate and actual Improved Buoyancy Tool integration tests also pass.
This is not a live constrained-boat test. Pitch/roll, flexible constraints and
framework mass reporting still require testing on your craft; no guarantee that
all sources of bouncing are eliminated. Source native water remains unimplemented.

Includes all previous map, ACF, waterjet, material, buoyancy tool and E2 hologram fixes.
