REVISION 19 - DISPLACEMENT AND VSS BUOYANCY
Replace the previous combined addon folder, fully restart and reconnect clients.

Ordinary props now displace water according to collision volume, submerged
fraction and buoyancy ratio rather than receiving lift proportional to their
own mass. Low-density floatable props such as barrels can float; sufficiently
heavy/dense props sink. Increasing mass does not increase displacement.
Explicit tool ratios are retained even on branches without GetBuoyancyRatio.
Without an accessible ratio, ordinary props use 1; engine-computed material
ratios cannot be recovered exactly on those branches.

Eight equal-volume OBB cells provide smooth approximate immersion and distributed
lift. Drag uses velocity at each submerged point, opposing translation and rocking
without directly changing angular velocity. Zero buoyancy disables lift, not drag.
Native brush water is excluded to avoid applying water forces twice.

VSS acf_buoyancy compartments are explicitly included. Uses MaxBuoyancyMass and
its existing SetBuoyancyRatio output, including Wire percentage and health.
The fork maps 100 percent to 100/60; the adapter uses the corresponding 0.6 scale
so full immersion at 100 percent supplies the published capacity. Requires a
naval baseplate just as the fork does. Does not replace the fork's entity Think,
input actions, damage code or deletion rules. ACF must be installed separately.

This is a Lua approximation of native water, not exact VPhysics parity: OBB
immersion approximates irregular/hollow models; missing physics volume falls
back to bounding-box volume, and drag has not been calibrated on a live server.
WaterLevel/contents, native splashes and native engine vehicle-water controllers
are not emulated by this force adapter. ACF waterjets are outside this change.

Validation: actual water hook tested for mass/volume displacement, zero-ratio drag,
dry/frozen/native-water exclusions, rotated/partial immersion, rotational drag,
and settling at 30/66/100 Hz. The actual unmodified local VSS buoyancy Think and
UpdateBuoyancyRatio were executed against the adapter: health and disconnected
baseplate behavior pass. Tests use a barrel-density proxy, not a live model.
Live barrel and naval craft comparisons against a regular water map are needed.
