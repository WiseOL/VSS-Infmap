REVISION 24 - WATERJET RESISTANCE
Replace the old combined addon folder, restart fully and reconnect.

The InfMap jet bridge retains VSS's original Calc/Act thrust. However the Lua
water adapter applied a 2/second horizontal damping rate to every submerged hull
prop/compartment. A fully submerged free body retained only exp(-2), about 13.5%,
of its horizontal velocity after one second under that drag alone. This is a
plausible contributor to weak waterjet propulsion, not a measured live diagnosis.

Naval-baseplate-connected hull props/compartments now use a separate adjustable
horizontal damping rate, default 0.15/second. Vertical resistance and the v23
shared compartment heave damping remain independent. Ordinary non-naval props
retain their previous drag. VSS thrust, RPM, gearing, damage and health rules
are not boosted or changed. Horizontal yaw resistance may also be reduced.

Server console / server.cfg:
infmap_water_naval_drag 0.15
Range 0-5. Lower means less horizontal resistance. Zero disables this adapter's
horizontal naval drag, but retains vertical resistance. Settings are archived.
For comparison with the old horizontal rate: infmap_water_naval_drag 2
These are approximate Lua damping settings, not native Source water coefficients.
Requires a VSS naval baseplate association. Craft without that association retain
ordinary prop drag. Jet weakness from torque/gearing/configuration remains possible.

Tests: actual water hook coast behavior matches configured damping at 30/66/100Hz;
zero horizontal damping retains vertical resistance; non-naval behavior preserved.
Loaded craft v23 heave tests still pass. Actual VSS jet Calc/Act/Think tests pass.
No live-server top-speed or acceleration measurement was performed.
Includes all previous combined map/ACF/E2/material/tool compatibility fixes.
