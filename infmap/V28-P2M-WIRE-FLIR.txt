REVISION 28 - P2M COMPLETE CHUNK DRAW / WIRE FLIR RENDER OWNERSHIP
Replace the previous combined addon folder, fully restart the server, and ALL
clients must disconnect/reconnect. Do not hot-reload into the old render chain.
Keep ACF-Team Prop2Mesh and Wiremod installed separately.
https://github.com/ACF-Team/Prop2Mesh

RENDER RECURSION
The old low-LOD wrapper called self.OldRenderOverride dynamically. P2M refresh,
chunk wrapper rebuilds and FLIR backup/restores could replace that field with
another InfMap wrapper or nil, causing the reported stack overflow/nil call.
New client render bridge captures the underlying draw callback, tracks its own
wrapper identities with weak tables, and blocks reentrant drawing per entity.
Temporarily removes RenderOverride for native DrawModel calls and restores it,
the camera frame and busy state even when the callback throws. Errors are logged
at most once per five seconds per entity rather than continuously recursing.

P2M GEOMETRY AND CHUNKS
Uses each controller's complete Draw function, including its split/complex mesh
loop, instead of calling only the native model/clip override. Controller link
position/angle remain parent-local, while chunk metadata follows the physical
root. One camera shift is applied to the viewer-relative chunk difference.
Clip planes wrap the entire controller draw in the matching native frame.
Scaled mesh bounds come from prop2mesh.recycle geometry, including negative
scale and geometry rebuilds. Does not judge a large P2M by the tiny placeholder
model's BoundingRadius or replace it with the ordinary distant box LOD. The
existing overall 100-chunk rendering-distance limit remains.

WIRE FLIR
Patches Wire's client FLIR.Render only on active InfMap maps. Keeps FLIR as the
outer pass and updates its BackupRenderOverride when chunks change. Calls the
chunk-managed complete draw exactly once instead of backing up a wrapper and
then drawing a second native-position copy. Honors FLIR.hide, stack membership,
material/gain controls and start/stop. Handles P2M refresh while FLIR is active.
The installed Wiremod lua/wire/flir.lua was inspected and used in tests.

This is a client rendering correction, not a P2M server/network rewrite. It does
not reconstruct meshes whose downloads have failed, transmit dormant/missing
link entities, or disable world occlusion/depth. Those would require further
diagnosis if parts are still absent after reconnecting.
