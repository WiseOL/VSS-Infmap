REVISION 18 - MESH WATER MATERIAL
Replaces the direct Hammer Water shader assignment from v17 with a bundled
Refract material for the Lua water mesh. Reuses dev/dev_water2_cheap normal/bump
texture if mounted and valid; otherwise retains the included animated normal map.
Restores the original dedicated underwater overlay. Refreshes the refraction
texture once before both surface draws and skips sky/depth passes.
This adapts ripple appearance; it does not reproduce brush-water reflections,
fog or the full Hammer material. Physics and ballistic monitoring are unchanged.
Replace the previous addon folder, fully restart and reconnect clients.
Mocked normal-map selection/fallback and existing buoyancy regression tests pass.
Visual appearance still requires in-game verification.
