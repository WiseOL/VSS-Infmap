REVISION 25 - AIRBOAT FLOTATION / HL2 WHEEL DIAGNOSTICS
Replace the previous combined addon folder, fully restart and reconnect.

Airboat: prop_vehicle_airboat now has a dedicated mass-supported hull flotation
path (full immersion supports twice its own weight at ratio 1), samples the lower
45 percent of local bounds to exclude the tall fan, and uses vertical spring
damping. This substitutes for the native vehicle water controller, which does
not recognize InfMap's rendered plane. Does not force native water contents or
restore the full engine airboat controller. Explicit tool ratios, including zero,
are honored; an unmodified engine default zero does not disable the adapter.
Buoyancy tool/movable/native-water exclusions remain. Live handling unverified.

HL2 WHEELS ARE NOT FIXED IN THIS BUILD.
Their native vehicle contact path is distinct from Lua traces. There is no
verified engine-compatible terrain contact correction yet; no wheel force,
collision enabling, hidden moving ground or general trace detour was added.

With a spawned jeep on the affected terrain, run as superadmin/server console:
infmap_hl2_vehicle_status
Saves data/infmap/hl2_vehicle_latest.json on the SERVER and prints its contents.
Please provide that file. Captures at most 16 prop_vehicle_* entities, up to 16
wheels each, native contact states, wheel collision/game flags, chunk/global
positions and native downward terrain traces. Native trace and contact readings
are diagnostic; they do not prove the engine collision path is fixed.

Tests: actual water hook airboat proxy settles at 30/66/100Hz even with zero native
ratio and tiny physical volume. Explicit zero and native-water exclusions pass.
Loaded craft and naval drag regressions pass. Diagnostic command compiles and
permission/empty-result save tests pass. Tests use mocked GMod physics APIs.
Includes all v24 combined map, ACF and Wire hologram compatibility fixes.
